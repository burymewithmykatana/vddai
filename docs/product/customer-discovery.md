# Customer Discovery

- Status: Draft research plan
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-10
- Evidence state: No customer interviews are recorded in this repository

## Objective

Validate whether a specific manufacturer has a repeated, costly visual
inspection problem that can support a constrained feasibility pilot.

## Hypotheses to Test

1. The inspection decision can be explained consistently by a domain expert.
2. Normal and defective examples can be collected under sufficiently stable
   capture conditions.
3. Missed defects, false rejection, rework, or manual inspection create
   measurable operational cost.
4. Human review is acceptable for uncertain cases during the pilot.
5. A successful pilot can lead to a clearly scoped production phase.

## Interview Guide

- What product and inspection point create the most recurring difficulty?
- How is acceptance decided today, and where do inspectors disagree?
- What defect types matter, and what is the consequence of missing each one?
- How many items are inspected and how much operator time is required?
- What images, defect examples, and historical decisions already exist?
- How stable are camera position, lighting, product presentation, and variants?
- What outcome would justify a pilot or a production investment?
- Who owns labeling, security, retention, integration, and final approval?

## Decision Rule

Advance an opportunity to a pilot proposal only when one inspection point has a
named operational owner, stable acceptance criteria, accessible representative
data, measurable consequences, and agreement on human oversight. Otherwise
record the blocking evidence and continue discovery.
